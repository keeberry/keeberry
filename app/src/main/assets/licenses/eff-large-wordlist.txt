EFF Large Wordlist for Passphrases

wordlists/eff_large.txt is the Electronic Frontier Foundation's large wordlist for passphrases,
published at https://www.eff.org/dice. KeeBerry leaves out the dice numbers and the four words
that contain a hyphen (drop-down, felt-tip, t-shirt and yo-yo), so a hyphen can separate words.
